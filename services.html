What we repair

Every job starts the same way: find the real fault, explain it in plain language, and quote it before touching anything.

Refrigeration

Fridges & freezers

A fridge that's drifting warm is a spoiling-food emergency, so these jobs get prioritised. Most cooling failures come down to a handful of causes — airflow, the defrost circuit, the thermostat, or a compressor that's struggling.

If the appliance still cools but not reliably, resist the urge to keep restarting it. Short-cycling a failing compressor is one of the few things that turns a repairable fridge into a replacement.

Book a fridge repair

Faults we see most

  • Fridge section warm while the freezer stays cold
  • Running constantly and never reaching temperature
  • Ice building up at the back of the fridge compartment
  • Water pooling under the salad drawers or on the floor
  • Loud buzzing, clicking, or a compressor that won't start
  • Door seals perished, torn, or no longer closing flush
Laundry

Washing machines

Washing machines fail loudly and inconveniently — mid-cycle, full of water, with a load of wet washing inside. Drainage faults and worn bearings account for a large share of the calls we take.

Before you book, it's worth checking the drain filter behind the bottom panel. Coins, buttons and hair clips block it constantly, and clearing it yourself may save you a call-out entirely.

Book a washer repair

Faults we see most

  • Water left standing in the drum at the end of a cycle
  • Drum won't spin, or spins only at low speed
  • Machine shakes hard enough to move across the floor
  • Grinding or rumbling on the spin cycle — usually bearings
  • Leaking from the door seal or from underneath
  • Cycle stops partway through and displays an error code
  • Door locked shut and refusing to release
Laundry

Tumble dryers

Dryers rarely fail outright — they get slower. If a load that used to take one cycle now takes three, the machine is telling you something, usually about restricted airflow or a heating element on its way out.

A dryer that smells hot or scorched should be unplugged and left alone until someone has looked at it. Lint is flammable, and overheating dryers are a genuine fire risk.

Book a dryer repair

Faults we see most

  • Tumbling normally but the clothes come out damp
  • No heat at all
  • Drum not turning — often a snapped belt
  • Cutting out partway through and refusing to restart
  • Squealing or scraping from the drum
  • Condenser models leaving water in the base
Kitchen

Dishwashers

When a dishwasher stops cleaning properly, the cause is nearly always water — not enough of it, not hot enough, or not being sprayed where it should be. Blocked spray arms and tired filters are the usual suspects.

Because dishwashers are plumbed in and often built into cabinetry, leaks can run for a long time before anyone notices. If you've seen water near the plinth, treat it as urgent — damaged flooring costs far more than the repair.

Book a dishwasher repair

Faults we see most

  • Dishes still dirty or gritty at the end of a cycle
  • Water sitting in the base once the cycle finishes
  • Not filling, or filling far too slowly
  • Nothing heating — everything comes out cold and wet
  • Leaking from the door or from under the unit
  • Detergent tablet left undissolved in the dispenser
Kitchen

Ovens, stoves & hobs

Cooking appliances fail in ways that are easy to work around and easy to ignore — one dead plate, a grill that no longer browns, a temperature that reads 180 and behaves like 140. They're also straightforward repairs in most cases.

If you can smell gas, don't book online. Shut off the supply, ventilate the room, and call your gas emergency service first.

Book an oven repair

Faults we see most

  • Oven not heating, or heating far below the set temperature
  • Food burning on one side and undercooked on the other
  • A single plate or ring that won't switch on
  • Grill element dead while the main oven still works
  • Thermostat overshooting or never cutting out
  • Door glass shattered, or hinges gone slack
  • Control panel unresponsive or displaying a fault code
Kitchen

Microwaves

Microwaves are worth a diagnosis before you replace them — but they're also the appliance most likely to come back as "not economical to repair", and we'll say so plainly when that's the case.

Never open a microwave yourself. The capacitor inside holds a dangerous charge long after it's been unplugged, and it can injure you badly.

Book a microwave repair

Faults we see most

  • Runs and lights up but doesn't heat
  • Arcing or sparking inside the cavity
  • Turntable not rotating
  • Door catch broken or not latching
  • Loud buzzing under load
  • Keypad or display unresponsive
The visit

What actually happens when we arrive

1. Diagnosis

The technician tests the appliance, isolates the fault, and explains what's failed and why — no jargon, no hand-waving.

2. Written quote

You get the cost of parts and labour in writing, plus an honest view on whether the repair is worth doing at all.

3. The repair

Once you approve, the work goes ahead — same visit where we're carrying the part, otherwise we order it and book a return.

Parts we don't carry on the van

No technician carries every component for every brand. If your repair needs a part ordered in, you'll be told the expected wait before you commit to anything — and the quote won't change once you've approved it.

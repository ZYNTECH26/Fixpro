About

Repair as a first option, not a last resort

Most household appliances get thrown away over a fault that costs a fraction of the replacement to fix. FixPro exists to make repairing the obvious choice — by being quick to reach, honest about cost, and clear about when a machine really is finished.

What we work on

Domestic kitchen and laundry appliances — fridges, freezers, washing machines, tumble dryers, dishwashers, ovens, hobs and microwaves — across most major brands.

How we charge

A call-out and diagnostic fee, confirmed when you book. Go ahead with the repair and that fee comes off the final invoice, so the diagnosis effectively costs you nothing.

What we guarantee

Three months on parts and labour. If the same fault comes back inside that window, so do we — and there's no second call-out fee.

How we quote

In writing, before anything is dismantled. If a second fault turns up mid-repair, work stops and you're asked again before the price moves.

How we work

Four things we hold to

The quote comes first

Nothing gets dismantled before you've seen what it costs. If the price changes because we find a second fault, work stops and you're asked again.

We'll talk you out of a bad repair

When the bill approaches what the appliance is worth, we say so. Losing a job is better than charging someone for a machine that fails again in six months.

Diagnose, don't guess

Swapping parts until the symptom disappears is expensive and slow. We test to find the actual failure, which usually means one part instead of three.

Realistic timing

You'll get an honest arrival window rather than an optimistic one, and a call if something slips. Waiting all day for a no-show is its own kind of damage.

Straight answers

Things worth knowing upfront

Is repairing actually cheaper than replacing?

Usually, but not always — and the honest answer depends on the appliance's age, the failed part, and what a like-for-like replacement costs. That's exactly the calculation we walk you through at the quote stage.

Do you use original manufacturer parts?

Where they're available and sensibly priced, yes. Where a quality equivalent costs considerably less, we'll offer you both options and let you choose — you'll always be told which one you're getting.

What if the same fault comes back?

Repairs carry a 3-month guarantee on parts and labour. If the fault we repaired returns within that period, we come back out and there's no second call-out fee.

Do you work on commercial appliances?

Domestic machines are the core of what we do, and they're what we're set up for. We do take on light commercial work — laundromats, guesthouses, small kitchens — where the appliance is a domestic-type machine. Get in touch with the make and model and we'll tell you straight whether it's something we can handle.

Got something that needs looking at?

Book it in, or call and we'll talk it through first.

What we fix

Six appliances, one number to call

If it lives in your kitchen or laundry and it's stopped behaving, it's probably on this list.

Fridges & freezers

Not cooling, freezing everything, leaking water, or running non-stop.

Washing machines

Won't drain, won't spin, walking across the floor, or flashing an error code.

Tumble dryers

Taking three cycles to dry, not heating, drum not turning, or overheating.

Dishwashers

Dishes coming out dirty, standing water in the base, or leaking onto the floor.

Ovens, stoves & hobs

No heat, wrong temperature, dead elements, or a plate that won't switch on.

Microwaves

Running but not heating, sparking inside, or a turntable that's given up.

How it works

Three steps, no guesswork

Tell us what's wrong

Book online or send a WhatsApp. Describe the fault, the brand, and roughly when it started — the more detail, the better prepared the technician arrives.

A technician inspects the appliance and gives you a written quote covering parts and labour. You decide whether to go ahead.

Questions

Before you book

What does a call-out cost?

The call-out and diagnostic fee is FILL IN: call-out fee, confirmed when you book so there are no surprises. If you go ahead with the repair, that fee comes off the final invoice.

How soon can someone come out?

It depends on the day and how far you are from us. Send your request through and we'll confirm the soonest realistic slot — we'd rather give you an honest time than a hopeful one.

Do you repair every brand?

We work on the vast majority of domestic brands. Some manufacturers restrict parts to their own service agents — if yours is one of them, we'll tell you upfront rather than waste your call-out fee.

What if it can't be fixed?

You'll get a straight answer and a written explanation of why. You still pay the diagnostic fee for the technician's time, but you won't be charged for a repair that was never going to work.

Do I need to be home?

Yes — someone over 18 needs to be there to give the technician access, approve the quote, and sign off the completed work.
